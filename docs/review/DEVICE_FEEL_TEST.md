# 10-minute iPhone feel test

**What this is:** the things the headless review could not measure, in order, in about 10 minutes.
**How to rate:** give each item a 1–5 rating (1 = bad, 3 = acceptable, 5 = great) and answer its question in a few words. Send this file back filled in.

**Setup (1 min)**
- Use the latest build: https://dormant-grid.dg-server.workers.dev. Add it to your Home Screen and launch it from there, in landscape.
- Settings: Quality **Auto**, Battery saver **Off**, Show performance stats **On**.
- Turn sound on with the ringer switch, at medium volume. Use headphones if you have them.

| # | Minute | Do this | Rate 1–5 | Answer briefly |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00 | Launch from the Home Screen. Watch the menu for 10 s. | Menu look | Is the whole **DORMANT** title visible, or cut off at the top? (The headless iPhone shot cuts it off.) |
| 2 | 0:15 | Tap **New Game** and watch the intro swoop. | Intro | Did it stutter? Count the hitches. |
| 3 | 0:45 | Hold the left side and walk in a circle for 10 s. | Walk start/stop | Does walking start the instant your thumb moves, and stop the instant you lift it? Did you ever drift after lifting? |
| 4 | 1:00 | Swipe the right side slowly, then flick it fast. | Camera turn | Slow swipe: too slow / right / too fast? Fast flick: does a full-width swipe turn about 180°? Did the camera ever jump? |
| 5 | 1:20 | Pinch to zoom in and out. Then walk next to a wall or tent and spin the camera around you. | Camera clipping | Did the camera go inside a wall, or did a bush or car block your character? (Headless shots show bushes and cars do block it.) |
| 6 | 1:45 | Walk to the marked crate. Hold the big **Break** button until it breaks. | Break juice | Felt satisfying (hitstop, shake, sound, chips flying in)? Did the button highlight the crate you meant? |
| 7 | 2:15 | Stand among 3–4 props close together. Turn slowly and watch which one the button names. | Targeting | Did it ever pick something **behind** you or beside you instead of the one in front? How often? |
| 8 | 2:45 | Open the pack, then Craft. Make a crowbar (break more crates if short). | UI legibility | Can you read every label at arm's length without squinting? Were any buttons hard to hit? |
| 9 | 3:30 | Look at a cookfire or a fire barrel for 5 s. | Smoke | Does the smoke look like smoke, or like grey cubes floating up? (Headless: cubes.) |
| 10 | 4:00 | Press **Build**. Place a foundation, then rotate and place a wall. | Build feel | Was it obvious where the piece would go and why it was red? Could you place it without precise dragging? |
| 11 | 5:00 | Stop and listen for 15 s at Haven (ambient, music, steps). | Sound | Does it sound like a place? What is missing (birds, wind, distant city)? Any crackle or clipping? |
| 12 | 5:30 | Sprint toward the tall buildings of downtown for about 60 s. Watch the stats overlay. | Smoothness | Lowest fps you saw while running? Any hitch when new streets appeared? Phone getting warm? |
| 13 | 6:30 | In downtown, stand still for 10 s and look around. | Mood | Does it feel tense or just empty? Name one thing that would make it feel dangerous. |
| 14 | 7:00 | If anything attacks, fight it with Swing and Roll. Otherwise swing at the air and roll 5 times. | Combat | Do hits land with weight? Is the roll responsive? Is the action button easy to find mid-fight? |
| 15 | 8:00 | Settings → set the day to 2 minutes. Wait for dusk and night (about 1 minute). | Night | Can you still see where to walk at night? Do the lit windows look warm or like flat stickers? |
| 16 | 9:00 | Lock the phone for 20 s, then unlock and return to the game. | Resume | Did it come back exactly where you were, without a reload? Any audio glitch? |
| 17 | 9:30 | Force-quit, relaunch, tap **Continue**. | Save | Is your crowbar and progress there? How many seconds from tap to playing? |
| 18 | 10:00 | Overall. | Would you play again tomorrow? | One sentence: what would make you open it tomorrow? |

**Also send back**
- **Settings → Copy device report**: paste it here.
- Your iPhone model and iOS version.
- Did the phone get warm? (no / slightly / hot)
- Battery used in 10 min, from Settings → Battery if you can.
