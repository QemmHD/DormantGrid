# Style cheat sheet (paste at the top of any ChatGPT chat)

Paste this block first, then a batch prompt from another sheet.

```
You are making game art for DORMANT GRID, a stylized
post-collapse town-rebuilding game. Style rules:
- Muted, gritty, stylized. NOT photoreal.
- Chunky simplified shapes, bevelled edges.
- Desaturated colors, strong light/dark contrast.
- Flat even lighting in textures. No baked shadows.
- One glow color only: Grid cyan-teal #39E6D0,
  used ONLY for power/Grid hardware.
- Ruins: cold blue-gray. Restored places: warm amber.
- No text, no logos, no brands, no watermark.
- No real-world trademarks or characters.
```

## Shared colors
- Grid glow: `#39E6D0`
- Grid dim: `#1D5A55`
- Danger red: `#E0533D`
- Warm lamp: `#FFB560`
- Cold light: `#9FB4C8`

## Don't
- No pure black or pure white.
- No neon apart from the Grid glow.
- No lens flare or chromatic aberration.
- No readable text in the art.
